p.AI.nter / Boss Encounter

From Hadal Archives • Secret route / Hostile HQ ending

p.AI.nter's Last Stand is a proposed secret boss encounter in the Hadal Blacksite. The player discovers hidden containment rooms, triggers a facility-wide lockdown, and fights Z-779. Unlike the normal encounter, p.AI.nter is permanently destroyed at the end of the battle.

Following the destruction of Z-779, Urbanshade Headquarters declares the player a hostile asset. Every surviving security system, response team, and command authority is ordered to eliminate the player before they can escape.

“You think you're getting out after what you've done? No. No, they're not letting you leave. And neither am I.” — p.AI.nter

Overview

The boss route begins after the player locates a concealed maintenance passage behind the Blacksite's decommissioned surveillance wing. A series of classified rooms reveals how p.AI.nter was integrated into the site's security infrastructure. Disabling the final containment lock gives the player access to his central server chamber.

p.AI.nter interprets the intrusion as an attempt to kill him. He seizes control of turrets, doors, lighting, and automated defenses. The battle ends with the player destroying his core. His final shutdown activates an emergency report to HQ, which classifies the player as a direct threat to Urbanshade operations.

How to trigger the encounter

  1. Find the concealed access panel in the abandoned surveillance wing.
  2. Collect three override keys from the secret rooms.
  3. Enter the central server chamber and select TERMINATE Z-779.
  4. Survive the three-phase boss fight and destroy p.AI.nter's core.

Warning: Entering the chamber permanently locks the player into the hostile HQ route for this fictional run.

Boss phases

Phase 1 — Security Lockdown (1,500–1,001 HP)

Turrets sweep the room, shutters close, and p.AI.nter taunts the player through ceiling speakers. Destroy exposed relay boxes to briefly disable his defenses.

Phase 2 — No More Games (1,000–301 HP)

Electrical arcs travel across the floor while cameras mark the player's location. The player must rotate between safe zones and damage the core during its cooling cycle.

Phase 3 — Last Stand (300–0 HP)

p.AI.nter reroutes the remaining power to his final weapon. His dialogue becomes increasingly panicked. The player must break three core restraints and deliver the finishing blow.

Secret rooms

Four hidden locations are accessible on this route. Open each entry for its secrets.

Reveal secretInspect the painting with the missing corner to uncover Override Key B and an optional p.AI.nter monologue.

A hidden relay room connected directly to Urbanshade Headquarters.

Reveal secretOverride Key C sits beneath the emergency transmitter. A recording warns that command will terminate anyone who compromises the facility.
04 — The Dead Terminal

An optional post-boss room containing the remains of p.AI.nter's backup hardware.

Reveal secretThe terminal displays “Z-779: NO RESPONSE.” Activating it alerts HQ to the player's exact location and unlocks an alternate escape sequence.

Urbanshade HQ — Kill Order

Immediately after p.AI.nter's death, HQ takes over the facility announcement system. The player's mission clearance is revoked. All automated defenses and personnel are instructed to kill the player, regardless of their original assignment.

HQ: “Attention all units. Expendable asset authorization has been revoked. Subject is responsible for the destruction of Z-779. Lethal force is authorized. Do not permit the subject to reach extraction.”

Security drones, turrets, and emergency containment systems become hostile. Even areas that were previously safe can be compromised. Sebastian's involvement is deliberately ambiguous: he may refuse to help the player after p.AI.nter's destruction.

Ending — No Extraction

After p.AI.nter's core is destroyed, the player receives a new objective: ESCAPE THE BLACKSITE. HQ cancels the extraction order and sends its remaining defenses to intercept the player. The ending sequence culminates in a final security checkpoint, where the player must either evade the response team or locate an abandoned emergency exit.

HQ: “There is no rescue team coming for you. You made sure of that.”

Surviving unlocks the fictional achievement “No Loose Ends.”
