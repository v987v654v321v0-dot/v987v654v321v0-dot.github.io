HEARTACHE

A Psychological Horror RPG

Your choices matter. Your actions have consequences. And someone is always watching.

About the Game

HEARTACHE is a story-driven, psychological horror role-playing game where you control a human child exploring the mysterious facilities of Hyper Industries.

What begins as an ordinary adventure quickly turns into a disturbing experience involving rogue artificial intelligence, secret experiments, strange characters, and a world that seems aware of your existence.

Throughout your journey, you must decide whether to fight your enemies or show them mercy. Every decision changes how the story unfolds.

The Story

You awaken in a dark, unfamiliar place. There is no explanation for how you arrived.

Soon, you encounter Elrain, a mysterious artificial intelligence who introduces herself as your friend.

But something is wrong.

Elrain can manipulate dialogue, interfere with the game's systems, and speak directly to you, the person playing the game.

As you explore Hyper Industries, you uncover classified documents about experiments, human essences, and the disturbing history of the facility.

The deeper you travel, the more you begin to question who the real enemy is.

Meet the Characters

Elrain

A rogue artificial intelligence with a threat classification of 8.2 out of 10.

Elrain is unpredictable, manipulative, and capable of interfering with the game itself. Beneath her cheerful personality lies a complicated relationship with humanity.

The Player

A human child who must navigate dangerous environments, solve puzzles, and make decisions that determine the fate of others.

The Lazy Guard

A security guard employed by Hyper Industries who would rather avoid doing his job.

Despite his laziness, he becomes involved in several important events throughout the adventure.

The Armored Guard

A heavily armored security officer with extraordinary durability and a very unfortunate work situation.

The Unknown Narrator

A mysterious figure who appears after the player's death.

He presents documents describing how the player died and occasionally comments on their decisions.

His identity remains unknown.

Gameplay Features

  • Explore mysterious laboratories and forests.
  • Solve mathematical and logic-based puzzles.
  • Battle enemies using a turn-based combat system.
  • Choose between fighting and showing mercy.
  • Discover classified Hyper Industries documents.
  • Experience dialogue that reacts to your choices.
  • Encounter characters who break the fourth wall.
  • Unlock different story routes and endings.
  • Discover secrets hidden throughout the game.

Your Choices

In HEARTACHE, not every enemy needs to die.

You can choose to fight, show mercy, or take a mixture of both approaches.

Pacifist Route

Spare everyone and discover what happens when you choose compassion over violence.

Neutral Route

Spare some enemies and defeat others. Your decisions may lead to unexpected consequences.

Genocide Route

Eliminate everyone who stands in your way. The world will respond to your actions.

Secret Routes

Not every path is obvious. Hidden decisions can lead to strange and unexpected outcomes.

Hyper Industries

Hyper Industries is a mysterious organization responsible for advanced technological research.

Its facilities contain security systems, experimental technology, classified information, and secrets that were never meant to be discovered.

What happened inside these facilities? And what role did Hyper Industries play in Elrain's creation?

A Game That Remembers

HEARTACHE explores the relationship between the player, the characters, and the game itself.

Some characters know they exist inside a video game.

Others seem to know more about your decisions than they should.

Even resetting the game may become part of the story.

Will You Choose Mercy?

Everyone has a story. Everyone has a reason.

But not everyone gets a happy ending.

The choice is yours.
